# QA Documentation & Language Policy
- All QA artifacts—including Test Plans, Acceptance Criteria in Specs, Bug Reports, and test files (`*.test.*`, `*.spec.*`)—must be written strictly in clear, professional **English**.
- When reviewing or generating Acceptance Criteria inside `.kiro/specs/`, always append a dedicated **Edge Cases & Negative Scenarios** subsection in English before finalizing the specification.
