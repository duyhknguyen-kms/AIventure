# AIventure
## AI Smart Job Aggregator & Matching Platform
## Overview:
An intelligent job search and matching platform that removes the hassle of manually browsing multiple recruitment sites. The system automatically scrapes and consolidates job listings across diverse web sources, analyzing job requirements and company benefits against user-defined criteria to surface the highest-matching opportunities.
What outcome do you expect by the end of the 2 days?
A functional Proof of Concept (PoC) MVP featuring:
Multi-Source Web Data Aggregator: A functional data ingestion pipeline that scrapes and normalizes job postings from multiple job boards.
AI Job-Matching & Benefit Analysis Engine: A smart filter that evaluates job listings, company benefits, and requirements against user criteria to score and surface the best-fit positions.
Job Recommendation Dashboard: An intuitive dashboard displaying curated job suggestions, key company benefit breakdowns, and match percentages tailored to the user.
