### Summary

The requirements document is well-structured and testable at the sub-requirement level, covering all three enhancement areas (CV parsing, AI matching, QA layer) with clear Given/When/Then acceptance criteria throughout FR1.1–FR1.4, FR2.1–FR2.4, FR3.1–FR3.4, and all five NFRs. Two major findings need resolution before user stories can be written without ambiguity: the undefined UI-vs-code meaning of "configurable" in the AI score blend weight (FR2.1/Q4), and the missing user-experience specification for when the per-session LLM call cap is exhausted mid-browsing (NFR3). The three minor findings are advisory — they will improve testability and ToS defensibility but do not block the user stories stage from proceeding once the two major items are addressed.
